# Page Spec: Mission & Vision

**URL:** `/mission`
**Phase:** 1
**Template:** Interior (Standard)
**SEO Priority:** 0.8

---

## Purpose

The most vision-forward page on the site. This is where Eonrisia's ambition is fully expressed. It should leave the reader with a clear sense of what Eonrisia is trying to achieve over the next 10 years — and why it matters.

---

## Primary Audiences

| Audience | What They Need |
|---|---|
| Partners / Donors | Understand the long-term vision to evaluate alignment |
| Potential contributors | Feel the weight of what they'd be joining |
| Press | Quotable, clear mission and vision statements |

---

## Sections

### 1. Page Hero
- Eyebrow: "Our Mission"
- Headline: The mission statement — bold, one or two sentences
- Subtext: One paragraph that grounds the statement

### 2. The Vision (Rich Text Block)
- Expands on the long-term 10-year vision
- What does success look like?
- Quote-worthy lines that could be used in press

### 3. How We Work (3-column block)
- Organization / Universe / Community — how the three layers relate
- Visual representation of the ecosystem structure

### 4. The Token System (Rich Text Block)
- Briefly explains the contribution-reward model
- How community members earn recognition and benefits by contributing
- Kept high-level — not a technical spec

### 5. Growth Phases (Timeline or Steps Block)
- Phase 1: Build visibility
- Phase 2: Recruit contributors
- Phase 3: Release products
- Phase 4: Sustain and grow
- Indicates current phase

### 6. CTA Band
- "Want to help build this?" headline
- "Volunteer" + "Partner With Us" buttons

---

## SEO

- **Title:** `Mission & Vision | Eonrisia`
- **Meta Description:** `Eonrisia's mission is to build one of the most ambitious community-driven fictional universes. Read our vision, values, and long-term goals.`
- **H1:** Set via CMS (the mission statement)
