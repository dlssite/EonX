# Page Spec: About

**URL:** `/about`
**Phase:** 1
**Template:** Interior (Standard)
**SEO Priority:** 0.8

---

## Purpose

Build trust and credibility with first-time visitors who want to understand who Eonrisia is before they engage further. This page is where partners, donors, and press go to understand the organization's identity and story.

---

## Primary Audiences

| Audience | What They Need |
|---|---|
| Partners / Donors | Understand the org's legitimacy and scope |
| Press | Quick, authoritative reference for who Eonrisia is |
| Potential volunteers | Connect emotionally with the mission |

---

## Sections

### 1. Page Hero
- Eyebrow: "About Eonrisia"
- Headline: Communicates the org's identity in one bold line
- Lead paragraph: 2–3 sentences expanding the headline
- No CTA in hero (let the content breathe)

### 2. Our Story (Rich Text Block)
- Narrative of how Eonrisia came to be
- Focus on the *why* — not a timeline of features
- 3–4 paragraphs max

### 3. What We Do (Stats / Icons Block)
- 3 columns: Organization (manage + recruit + govern) / Universe (Sanctyria and future universes) / Community (the people who build it)
- Brief description of each pillar

### 4. Our Values (Rich Text or Stats Block)
- 4–5 core values with a short sentence each
- Bold value name + explanatory sentence

### 5. Team Preview (Team Block)
- "Meet our core team" section heading
- 4–6 team member cards
- "See full team →" link

### 6. CTA Band
- "Want to be part of this?" headline
- Two buttons: "Volunteer" + "Contact"

---

## SEO

- **Title:** `About Eonrisia — Community-Driven Creative Organization`
- **Meta Description:** `Learn about Eonrisia — the organization building community-driven fictional universes. Our mission, story, team, and values.`
- **H1:** Set in page hero via CMS
- **Structured Data:** BreadcrumbList
