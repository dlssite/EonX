# Brand Identity — Eonrisia

## Organization Name
**Eonrisia**

## Tagline (working)
> "Building immersive worlds, together."

---

## What Eonrisia Is

Eonrisia is a community-driven creative organization. It builds immersive, self-sustaining fictional universes and the software, games, comics, music, and community infrastructure that brings them to life. Its core team is lean and works with volunteers and partner teams to achieve its goals.

Eonrisia is **not** a product company. It is not a game studio alone. It is not a publisher alone. It is an ecosystem builder — the organization that manages and grows a creative community at scale.

---

## Brand Pillars

These four pillars define every Eonrisia communication and design decision.

### 1. Ambitious
We are building something genuinely large. The visual and written identity must communicate scale, vision, and long-term thinking. We do not look like a hobby project.

### 2. Credible
We have standards. Our work is considered, structured, and delivered with intention. We look and sound like people who know exactly what they are doing.

### 3. Community-first
The ecosystem exists because of the people who build it. The brand feels like a place people belong to — not a corporation looking down at them.

### 4. Original
We are not derivative. Eonrisia and Sanctyria are original IP. The brand must feel like nothing else in the market.

## Tone of Voice

| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Hero headlines | Bold, declarative, short. No filler. |
| Mission / About copy | Considered, visionary, grounded in real intent |
| Volunteer / contributor pages | Warm, inviting, peer-to-peer — not corporate HR |
| Partner / donor pages | Credible, direct, outcome-focused |
| Commission / work with us | Confident, capable, collaborative |
| Error pages / edge states | Human, calm, helpful |

### Voice Principles
- **Say it once, say it well.** No padding, no repetition.
- **Active voice.** "We build" not "We are focused on building."
- **Specific over vague.** "A living fictional universe with games, comics, and community tools" not "an innovative creative platform."
- **Never oversell.** The work speaks. The copy frames it.

---

## Brand Relationship to Sanctyria

Eonrisia is the organization. Sanctyria is the universe Eonrisia builds.

On the Eonrisia organization website:
- Sanctyria is referenced as Eonrisia's flagship universe — a real project with real scope.
- Lore, characters, nations, and world-building content do **not** live here. That belongs on the Sanctyria universe website.
- The relationship is similar to how a film studio's website mentions a major franchise without containing the franchise's full wiki.

## Logo

See [`logo-usage.md`](./logo-usage.md) for full usage rules.

- The primary logo is the **wordmark**: "Eonrisia" in the brand typeface.
- A secondary **symbol/mark** may be developed for use at small sizes and as a favicon.
- Never stretch, recolor, or add effects to the logo.
