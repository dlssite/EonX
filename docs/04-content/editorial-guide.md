# Editorial Guide — Eonrisia

## Purpose

This guide governs all written content published on the Eonrisia organization website. It ensures the brand voice is consistent regardless of who is writing.

---

## Voice & Tone Summary

**Voice** is who we are. It does not change.
**Tone** shifts based on context.

| We always are: | We never are: |
|---|---|
| Direct | Vague or evasive |
| Confident | Arrogant or dismissive |
| Warm | Corporate or cold |
| Ambitious | Hyperbolic or overselling |
| Clear | Jargon-heavy or unnecessarily complex |

---

## Writing Principles

### 1. Lead with the point
Put the most important information first. Don't make the reader dig for it.

**Wrong:** "At Eonrisia, we have been working hard over the past few years to build something that we believe can make a real difference in the creative community."

**Right:** "We build living fictional universes — and the community infrastructure that keeps them alive."

---

### 2. Use active voice
Subject does the action. Always.

**Wrong:** "Volunteers are supported by our team."

**Right:** "Our team supports every volunteer."

---

### 3. Be specific
Vague language loses trust. Specifics earn it.

**Wrong:** "We work on many exciting creative projects."

**Right:** "We're currently building Sanctyria — a community-driven fictional universe with games, comics, and original lore."

---

### 4. Write for scanning
Most visitors scan before they read. Use short paragraphs (3 sentences max), subheadings, and bullet points where appropriate.

---

### 5. Earn every word
If a word doesn't add meaning, remove it. This applies to:
- Filler phrases: "In order to", "It is important to note that", "As mentioned above"
- Weak intensifiers: "very", "really", "quite", "extremely"
- Redundant pairs: "each and every", "first and foremost"

## Page-by-Page Tone Guide

### Home
- Bold, declarative headline. Short. No filler.
- Subheadline expands the headline in one sentence.
- CTAs are action verbs: "Join Us", "See Our Work", "Get Involved"

### About
- Narrative but purposeful. Tell the story without being self-congratulatory.
- Focus on *why* Eonrisia exists, not just *what* it does.
- Acknowledge the community. They are not just users — they are co-builders.

### Mission & Vision
- The most vision-forward page. This is where ambition is fully expressed.
- Short, powerful sentences. Each one should land.
- Avoid clichés: "change the world", "disrupt", "passion-driven"

### Team
- Bio tone: third-person, professional but human. Max 2 sentences.
- Focus on what the person brings, not their full resume.

### Volunteer
- Warm, peer-to-peer tone. "You" and "we" — not "applicants" or "candidates."
- Be specific about what volunteers will actually do and what they'll get from it.
- No corporate HR language.

### Projects
- Outcomes first. What exists or will exist because of this project?
- Status transparency: if something is in early development, say so.

### Work With Us (Commission)
- Confident and collaborative — we know what we're doing.
- Focus on what we can build *for them*, not our own achievements.
- End every section with a clear next action.

### Contact
- Short and direct. Tell them what to expect after submitting.
- "We typically respond within 2–3 business days."

## Formatting Rules

- **Headings:** Sentence case (not Title Case). "Our team" not "Our Team"
- **Lists:** Use bullet points for 3+ parallel items. Use prose for 1–2 items.
- **Numbers:** Spell out one through nine. Use numerals for 10+. Exception: stats ("50+ contributors", "3 active projects")
- **Dates:** Month D, YYYY format ("August 28, 2026"). No ordinals ("August 28th").
- **Ampersand (&):** Only in navigation and UI labels. Never in body copy.
- **Ellipsis:** Avoid in body copy. Acceptable in UI loading states.

## Words to Avoid

| Avoid | Use instead |
|---|---|
| Leverage | Use |
| Utilize | Use |
| Synergy | Collaboration |
| Ecosystem (when vague) | Be specific |
| Passionate | Dedicated, committed |
| Exciting | Be specific about what's notable |
| World-class | Let the work speak |
| Innovative | Describe the innovation |
