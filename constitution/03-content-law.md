# Content Law — Eonrisia Constitution

These rules govern what content may and may not appear on the Eonrisia organization website.

---

## What the Org Site Is For

The Eonrisia organization website exists to serve:
1. Partners and donors evaluating the organization
2. Contributors and volunteers considering joining
3. Commission clients evaluating Eonrisia as a team to hire
4. Press and media covering Eonrisia

Every page, section, and piece of content must serve at least one of these four audiences.

---

## What Must Always Be Present

- A clear statement of what Eonrisia is, on the home page, visible without scrolling
- Contact information or a path to contact the organization
- An up-to-date team page (never more than 90 days stale if team composition has changed)
- An open volunteer roles section (even if current answer is "no open roles")
- Legal/copyright notice in the footer

---

## What Is Permitted

- Organization news, updates, and milestone announcements
- Team member profiles and bios
- Project showcase (descriptions and links only — no lore)
- Volunteer and contributor recruitment content
- Commission and partnership information
- Governance and transparency content
- Press kit and media resources
- Donation information
- External links to Eonrisia social profiles and the Sanctyria universe site

---

## What Is Prohibited

The following content may never appear on the Eonrisia organization website:

- **Universe lore, fiction, or character content** from Sanctyria or any other universe. This belongs on the universe website.
- **Unverified claims** — statistics, team sizes, impact numbers that cannot be substantiated
- **Political commentary** not directly related to the organization's mission
- **Third-party advertising** or sponsored content that is not a formal partnership
- **Content promoting specific individuals** outside of their role at Eonrisia
- **Placeholder or "coming soon" pages** visible to the public — build it when it's ready, or don't show it
- **Thin content pages** — any page that goes live must have substantive content. No skeleton pages with filler text.

---

## Content Freshness

- Home page hero copy must be reviewed every 6 months
- Team page must reflect current team composition within 30 days of a change
- Open volunteer roles must be reviewed and updated every 30 days
- Blog posts (Phase 2) older than 2 years must be reviewed and either updated or archived

---

## Legal and Compliance

- All content on the site is the intellectual property of Eonrisia
- No third-party content (images, text, video) may be used without a verified license or explicit permission
- All stock photography must have a commercial license on file
- The footer must always include a copyright notice: `© [Year] Eonrisia. All rights reserved.`
