# Mission & Values — Eonrisia Constitution

## The Mission

Eonrisia exists to build community-driven creative ecosystems — living, self-sustaining universes that grow through the collective effort of writers, artists, developers, musicians, and fans.

The organization does not exist for profit. It exists to make something lasting, ambitious, and genuinely communal.

---

## The Vision

In ten years, Eonrisia should be known as the community that built one of the most ambitious free fictional universes together — not as the company that made a cool product.

The books, games, apps, and comics are not the destination. They are the ways people experience and contribute to the same world.

---

## Core Values

These five values govern every decision made in the name of Eonrisia — including every decision about this website.

### 1. Community First
The ecosystem exists because of the people who build it. No decision that benefits the organization should come at the expense of the community's trust, access, or experience.

### 2. Transparency
We operate openly. How we make decisions, how we use resources, and who is accountable for what — these things are not hidden. Transparency is the foundation of trust.

### 3. Quality Over Speed
We ship things when they are ready, not when they are due. A site that looks unfinished or under-resourced signals that the work inside the organization is also unfinished. Standards are maintained even when it is inconvenient.

### 4. Ownership
We own our tools, our data, and our decisions. We do not build on platforms that can pull the rug. We choose open-source and self-hosted solutions where practical.

### 5. Originality
Eonrisia and its universes are original. The brand, the design, and the work must feel like nothing else. Derivative aesthetics, generic templates, and borrowed ideas are not acceptable for the official face of this organization.

---

## What the Website Must Always Reflect

- The website is the official face of the organization — it must maintain the dignity, ambition, and credibility of that role at all times.
- The website serves the community, volunteers, partners, and donors — not internal organizational ego.
- The website's quality sets the standard for everything else Eonrisia produces.
