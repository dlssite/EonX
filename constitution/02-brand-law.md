# Brand Law — Eonrisia Constitution

These rules govern the Eonrisia brand on the official website. Violations require immediate correction. Changes to these rules require the amendment process.

---

## 1. The Name

The organization name is **Eonrisia**. It is written:

- **Correct:** Eonrisia
- **Wrong:** Eonrisia, EON RISEN, Eonrisia (in body copy), Eonrisia (except in all-caps logo lockups where design dictates)

---

## 2. The Logo

- The primary logo is the Eonrisia wordmark
- The wordmark and symbol mark may never be stretched, distorted, recolored outside approved variants, or decorated with effects
- No third party may create unofficial variations of the Eonrisia logo
- The logo must always have minimum clear space (see `docs/01-brand/logo-usage.md`)
- The logo is never used as a typographic element — it is a brand mark

---

## 3. The Color System

- The Eonrisia brand color is `#6C63FF` (brand-500). This value is fixed.
- Raw hex values may never be hardcoded in components — all colors are referenced via CSS custom property tokens
- The color palette defined in `docs/01-brand/color-system.md` may not be overridden per page or per component without a documented design decision
- The brand gradient (`brand-500 → accent-500`) is the only permitted multi-color gradient

---

## 4. The Typefaces

- **Syne** is the exclusive display and heading typeface for the Eonrisia website. No other typeface may be used for headings.
- **Inter** is the exclusive body and UI typeface. No other typeface may be used for body text.
- Additional typefaces may only be introduced through the amendment process.

---

## 5. Tone of Voice

- The Eonrisia website never uses: hype language, superlatives without specific evidence, corporate filler phrases, or passive voice in primary copy.
- Headlines are declarative, direct, and short.
- The voice never changes based on who is writing — every page sounds like the same Eonrisia.
- See `docs/04-content/editorial-guide.md` for the full voice guide.

---

## 6. Brand Separation from Sanctyria

- The Eonrisia organization website and the Sanctyria universe website are separate brand properties.
- Lore, fiction, character names, world-building content, and universe-specific visual language from Sanctyria do not appear on the org site.
- Sanctyria is referenced only as a project, with a name, description, and link to the universe site.
- This separation must never be compromised to generate universe-fan traffic on the org site.
